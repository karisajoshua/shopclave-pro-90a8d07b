# Complete the product review presentation

- Keep genuine submitted reviews first and preserve their existing “Verified buyer” label.
- When fewer than five genuine reviews exist, show polished sample review cards to complete the visual layout, clearly labeled “Sample review” and using generic anonymous names so they cannot be mistaken for real customer testimonials.

## Verification
- Add focused checks for distribution totals, real-review priority, truthful labels, and the five-card presentation.
- Run the relevant tests and inspect the review section on mobile.

## Scope safeguards
- Do not create review records, customer accounts, comments, or identities in the database.
